# Research answer

**Question:** Is the member enquiry AI pilot ready for a wider launch? What conditions are still outstanding?

The member enquiry AI pilot is not yet ready for a wider launch. The board's August 2026 approval was subject to three conditions, and at least one remains unmet [1].

1. **Australian data residency** — This condition appears satisfied. The pilot ran on an Australian-hosted model service and no member data left Australia during the pilot [2].

2. **Staff review of every answer** — The board required that every answer sent to a member be reviewed by a staff member before sending [1]. The pilot results do not explicitly confirm whether this review process was operationally in place, so this condition cannot be verified from the available documents.

3. **Risk Committee accuracy threshold** — This condition is not met. The Risk Committee set a threshold of at least 95% accuracy on a test set of 500 real member enquiries [3]. The pilot achieved 93.4% accuracy (467 out of 500 correct), falling short of the required 95% [2][3]. Most errors involved recent changes to contribution caps [2].

In summary, the outstanding condition is the accuracy threshold: the pilot's 93.4% accuracy is below the 95% required by the Risk Committee [2][3]. Until that gap is closed and the Risk Committee confirms the threshold is met, a wider launch cannot proceed [1]. The staff-review condition is also not explicitly confirmed in the pilot results, so its status remains unclear from the available documents.
