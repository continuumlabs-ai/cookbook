# Research answer

**Question:** Is the member enquiry AI pilot ready for a wider launch? What conditions are still outstanding?

The member enquiry AI pilot is **not yet ready** for a wider launch. The board's August 2026 approval was subject to three conditions, and at least one remains unmet [1][2].

**The three conditions and their status:**

1. **Member data stored and processed in Australia** — This condition appears to be satisfied. The pilot ran on an Australian-hosted model service and no member data left Australia during the pilot [4].

2. **Every answer reviewed by a staff member before sending** — This requirement is established in the AI use policy, which states that any AI-generated content sent to a member must be reviewed and approved by a staff member first [5]. The pilot results describe senior staff marking draft answers, consistent with human review being in place [3].

3. **Risk Committee confirmation of accuracy threshold** — This is the **outstanding condition**. The Risk Committee set the threshold as follows: "The assistant must answer at least 95% of a test set of 500 real member enquiries correctly, as judged by senior staff" [6]. The October 2026 pilot results show that "Senior staff marked the assistant's draft answers to 500 member enquiries. 467 were correct, an accuracy of 93.4%" [3], which falls short of the 95% threshold [6]. Most errors involved recent changes to contribution caps [7].

**Conclusion:** The pilot has not met the accuracy threshold required by the Risk Committee, so the committee cannot yet confirm readiness for a wider launch [1][6]. Until accuracy improves to at least 95% [6], the pilot should not proceed to a wider launch. The other two conditions (Australian data residency and human review) appear to be in place [4][5].
