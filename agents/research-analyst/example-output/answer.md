# Research answer

**Question:** Is the member enquiry AI pilot ready for a wider launch? What conditions are still outstanding?

Not yet. The board made any wider launch conditional on the Risk Committee confirming the accuracy threshold [1]. The committee set that threshold at 95% of 500 real member enquiries answered correctly [2]. The pilot scored 93.4% [3], so the threshold has not been met.

The other two board conditions are in hand. Member data must stay in Australia [4], and no member data left Australia during the pilot [5]. Every answer must be reviewed by staff before it is sent [6]. The documents do not say how that review performed at scale.

The committee also asked for the hand-off rate [7]. The pilot handed 12% of enquiries to a person [8].
