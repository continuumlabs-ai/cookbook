# Risk Committee minutes, September 2026

Banksia Super (fictional). Meeting held on 22 September 2026.

## AI pilot accuracy threshold

The committee discussed the risk of the AI assistant giving members incorrect information.

The committee set the accuracy threshold for launch. The assistant must answer at least 95% of a test set of 500 real member enquiries correctly, as judged by senior staff.

The committee also asked management to report how often the assistant hands an enquiry to a person.
