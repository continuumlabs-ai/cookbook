# Pilot results, October 2026

Banksia Super (fictional). Prepared for the Risk Committee.

## Accuracy

Senior staff marked the assistant's draft answers to 500 member enquiries. 467 were correct, an accuracy of 93.4%.

Most errors involved recent changes to contribution caps.

## Efficiency

Average handling time per enquiry fell by 38%.

## Hand-offs

The assistant handed 12% of enquiries to a person.

## Hosting

The pilot runs on an Australian-hosted model service. No member data left Australia during the pilot.
