# AI use policy

Banksia Super (fictional). Version 2.1, approved June 2026.

## Member information

Staff must not enter member personal information into any AI service that stores or processes data outside Australia.

## Human review

Any AI-generated content sent to a member must be reviewed and approved by a staff member first.

## Approved uses

AI may be used to draft replies to member enquiries, summarise internal documents and search the knowledge base. AI must not be used to give personal financial advice.
