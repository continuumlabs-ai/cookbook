# Board minutes, August 2026

Banksia Super (fictional). Minutes of the board meeting held on 14 August 2026.

## Member enquiry AI pilot

The board approved a pilot of an AI assistant to help staff answer member enquiries. The approved budget is $450,000 for the 2026–27 financial year.

Approval is subject to three conditions. Member data must be stored and processed in Australia. Every answer sent to a member must be reviewed by a staff member before it is sent. The Risk Committee must confirm the pilot meets its accuracy threshold before any wider launch.

Management will report progress to the Risk Committee by December 2026.
