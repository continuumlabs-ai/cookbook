# Contract review

The contract does not meet our policies as drafted. Seven of eight topics need changes, and three are high risk: offshore data, a three-month liability cap and the Supplier owning our deliverables. Do not sign until clauses 5, 6 and 7 are amended.

## Liability (clause 7)

Clause 7 caps the Supplier's liability at 3 months of fees and leaves the Customer's liability uncapped. Policy requires a cap of at least 12 months of fees and no uncapped liability for us.

Suggested wording:

> Each party's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid or payable in the 12 months before the claim.

## Data location (clause 5)

Data may be processed in Singapore or the United States, and backups are held in the United States. Policy requires all data and backups to stay in Australia.

Suggested wording:

> The Supplier will store and process all Customer data, including backups, only in Australia.

## Intellectual property (clause 6)

The Supplier keeps ownership of all deliverables and grants us only a licence for the term. Policy requires us to own deliverables created for us.

Suggested wording:

> The Customer owns all intellectual property in deliverables created specifically for it. The Supplier keeps its pre-existing tools and grants the Customer a perpetual licence to use them within the deliverables.

## Termination for convenience (clause 4)

We can terminate for convenience only on 180 days' notice. Policy allows at most 90 days.

Suggested wording:

> The Customer may terminate this agreement for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## Auto renewal (clause 3)

The agreement renews for 12-month terms unless notice is given 30 days before the end, and the Supplier has no duty to remind us. Policy requires a reminder at least 60 days before renewal.

Suggested wording:

> The Supplier must notify the Customer in writing at least 60 days before any renewal date. Without that notice, the agreement does not renew.

## Governing law (clause 9)

The agreement is governed by Singapore law. Policy requires Victoria or New South Wales.

Suggested wording:

> This agreement is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.

## Payment terms (clause 2)

Invoices are due within 14 days, and late payments attract 2% interest a month. Policy is 30 days. Shorter terms need Finance approval.

Suggested wording:

> The Customer will pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receiving it.
