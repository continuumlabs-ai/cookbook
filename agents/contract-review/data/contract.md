# Master Services Agreement

Between Harbourline Logistics Pty Ltd ("Customer") and Southgate Analytics Pty Ltd ("Supplier"). Both companies are fictional.

## 1. Services
The Supplier will provide route optimisation analytics and a monthly performance report, as described in Schedule A.

## 2. Fees and payment
The Customer will pay each invoice within 14 days of the invoice date. Late payments attract interest at 2% per month.

## 3. Term and renewal
This agreement starts on 1 January 2027 and runs for 24 months. It renews automatically for further 12-month terms unless either party gives notice at least 30 days before the end of the current term.

## 4. Termination
Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written notice if the breach is not remedied. The Customer may terminate for convenience on 180 days' written notice.

## 5. Data
The Supplier will host Customer data on its cloud platform. Data may be processed in Australia, Singapore or the United States, and backups are held in the United States.

## 6. Intellectual property
The Supplier owns all intellectual property in the analytics models, reports and other deliverables, and grants the Customer a non-exclusive licence to use them during the term.

## 7. Liability
The Supplier's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 3 months before the claim. The Customer's liability is not limited.

## 8. Confidentiality
Each party will keep the other's confidential information secret during the term and for 5 years after it ends.

## 9. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
