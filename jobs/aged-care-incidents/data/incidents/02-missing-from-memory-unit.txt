BANKSIA GROVE CARE (fictional) — INCIDENT REPORT IR-2614
Resident: R.T., Memory Support Unit
Reported by: Facility manager
Time staff became aware: 14/10/2026 15:05

At 15:00 staff noticed R.T. was not in the lounge. A search of the unit and grounds found the garden gate propped open by a contractor. Staff called police at 15:30 and reported R.T. missing. Police found R.T. at a bus stop 2 km away at 17:10. He was cold and confused but unhurt. He was checked by the RN on return and settled.

Contractor induction is being reviewed. Gate now alarmed.
