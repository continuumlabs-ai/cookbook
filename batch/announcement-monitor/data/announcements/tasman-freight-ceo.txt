Company: Tasman Freight Group (fictional)
Date: 15 October 2026
Title: Chief Executive Officer resignation

Tasman Freight Group announces that its Chief Executive Officer, who has led the company for six years, has resigned to pursue other opportunities. The resignation takes effect on 31 January 2027.

The board has started a search for a successor, considering internal and external candidates. The Chief Financial Officer will act as interim Chief Executive if a successor is not appointed by that date.
