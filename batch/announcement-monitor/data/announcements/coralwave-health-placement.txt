Company: Coralwave Health Ltd (fictional)
Date: 15 October 2026
Title: $85m placement to fund clinic acquisition

Coralwave Health has received firm commitments for an $85m placement to institutional investors at $1.42 per share, a 9.5% discount to the last closing price.

Proceeds will fund the acquisition of Northshore Clinics, a network of 14 day-surgery clinics, for $120m. The balance will be funded from existing debt facilities.

The acquisition is expected to be earnings per share accretive in its first full year of ownership.
