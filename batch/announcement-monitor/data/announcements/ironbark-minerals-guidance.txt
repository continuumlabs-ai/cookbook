Company: Ironbark Minerals Ltd (fictional)
Date: 15 October 2026
Title: Revised FY27 production guidance

Ironbark Minerals advises that a pit wall failure at its Kestrel mine on 9 October has closed the main haul road for an estimated eight weeks.

As a result, FY27 copper production guidance is reduced from 210-230kt to 180-195kt. All-in sustaining cost guidance increases by 12% to A$4.85/lb.

No one was injured. Remediation work has started and the company will provide an update at its quarterly report in January.
