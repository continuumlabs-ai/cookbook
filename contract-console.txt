# Contract review

This contract has significant compliance gaps across seven of eight policy topics, with high-risk exceptions in liability (3-month cap vs. required 12-month minimum, uncapped customer liability), data location (offshore processing in Singapore and the US without CRO approval), and intellectual property (supplier owns deliverables instead of the customer). Medium-risk issues include a 180-day termination-for-convenience notice (vs. 90-day maximum), no supplier renewal reminder obligation, and Singapore governing law (vs. required Victoria or NSW). Only the confidentiality clause (5-year survival) is compliant; all other clauses need redlines before execution.

7 of 8 policy topics need attention.

## Liability (clause 7)

Clause 7 caps the supplier's liability at 3 months of fees, but the policy requires a cap of no less than 12 months. Additionally, the customer's liability is uncapped, which the policy explicitly states is not acceptable.

Suggested wording:

> The Supplier's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. The Customer's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.

## Data location (clause 5)

Clause 5 permits processing in Singapore and the United States and holds backups in the United States, but the policy requires all customer data including backups to be stored and processed in Australia, with offshore processing only by written CRO approval.

Suggested wording:

> The Supplier will store and process all Customer data, including backups, in Australia. Any offshore processing or storage requires prior written approval from the Customer's Chief Risk Officer.

## Intellectual property (clause 6)

Clause 6 gives the supplier ownership of all IP in deliverables and grants only a licence to the customer, but the policy requires the customer to own all deliverables created specifically for it, with the supplier retaining only pre-existing tools.

Suggested wording:

> The Customer owns all intellectual property in the analytics models, reports and other deliverables created specifically for the Customer under this agreement. The Supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing tools, methodologies and intellectual property.

## Termination for convenience (clause 4)

Clause 4 allows the customer to terminate for convenience on 180 days' notice, but the policy requires the ability to terminate on no more than 90 days' notice.

Suggested wording:

> The Customer may terminate for convenience on 90 days' written notice.

## Auto renewal (clause 3)

Clause 3 provides for automatic renewal but imposes no obligation on the supplier to remind the customer at least 60 days before the renewal date, which the policy requires.

Suggested wording:

> This agreement renews automatically for further 12-month terms unless either party gives notice at least 30 days before the end of the current term. The Supplier must remind the Customer in writing at least 60 days before each renewal date.

## Governing law (clause 9)

Clause 9 designates Singapore law as the governing law, but the policy requires the contract to be governed by the laws of Victoria or New South Wales.

Suggested wording:

> This agreement is governed by the laws of Victoria.

## Payment terms (clause 2)

Clause 2 requires payment within 14 days of the invoice date, which is shorter than the policy's standard 30-day term and would require Finance approval.

Suggested wording:

> The Customer will pay each invoice within 30 days of receipt. Late payments attract interest at 2% per month.
